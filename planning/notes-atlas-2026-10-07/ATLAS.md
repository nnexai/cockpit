## Reading guide and evidence contract

This atlas is the navigable overview of **45 stable surface IDs**: 6 shell, 3 Scratchpad, 6 Todos, 4 comments, 9 Kanban, 8 Decisions and 9 recovery/storage. IDs identify surfaces or cross-cutting contracts, not separate routes. Shared controls intentionally appear under multiple IDs; they are not duplicated implementations.

- [Surface registry](#surface-registry): every slice ID, current owner/symbol and checked source range.
- [State/action matrices](#stateaction-matrices): empty, loading, disabled, error, pending, draft, conflict, unknown-outcome and removed-source behavior.
- [Navigation/focus map](#navigationfocus-map) and [preservation constraints](#persistence-identity-and-preservation-constraints).
- [Brief coverage](#brief-coverage) and [code-derived UX candidates](#code-derived-ux-candidates-for-design-review).
- [Visual evidence and observed verification](evidence/INDEX.md): maintained by the main investigator. Screenshot links below are an index, **not independent claims that an interaction worked**.

**Evidence classes must remain separate:**

1. **Code-derived:** statements in this atlas and all five slices, backed by inspected current source. They establish implemented branches/contracts, not observed browser/native behavior.
2. **Actual disposable application:** only an explicitly recorded observation in [evidence/INDEX.md](evidence/INDEX.md), with fixture/transport/context, can establish runtime behavior. A screenshot establishes its visible state, not every action leading to it, persistence across restart, concurrency safety or native IPC.
3. **Illustrative/synthetic:** design mockups or forced component states must be labeled as such. They are not current-app or live-service proof.
4. **Existing tests:** slice test references are useful coverage pointers; none were executed by this integration worker and test names are not run proof.

Full research: [shell/Scratchpad](slices/shell-scratchpad.md), [Todos/comments](slices/todos-comments.md), [Kanban](slices/kanban.md), [Decisions](slices/decisions.md), [recovery/storage](slices/recovery-storage.md). ## State/action matrices

All rows below are **code-derived**. A state not implemented locally is stated as absent/shared; do not fill it with an invented spinner, inline retry or auto-save. `busy` is the shared per-Notes-UUID mutation state, not a surface-specific save. “Unknown” means `notes_outcome_unknown`, never a confirmed failure. Ordinary errors include authoritative `notes_*` refusals, read errors and save-confirmed/refresh-failed; their recovery is not interchangeable.

### Cross-cutting gates

| State | Implemented response / permitted action | Stable surfaces |
