## Explicit verification limits

- Synthetic runtime DTOs and passing timer tests do not establish source-built native cadence. [NATIVE.md](NATIVE.md) separately retains Main's actual immutable-artifact native IPC cadence/immediate-refresh observations and trace inventory counts; CPU behavior/request-count performance remains **unverified here**, as does independent retirement execution.
- Real worker retirement, shutdown/process-exit identity, owned terminal/Space closure and preservation on the external/native lifecycle path are **not verified here**; that evidence belongs to the lifecycle worker. Displayed `native_stopped`/`retired` DTO states cannot substitute for it.
- No live provider writes, current user-session operations, Notes mutations or bindings are exercised by this evidence package. Startup/question/focus evidence is only from Main's named disposable fixture.
- Original startup UI/API acknowledgement and raw test/review logs are absent from the supplied inputs. They are not substituted by a fabricated receipt. The 22-scenario matrix is complete for its stated browser consumer scope; native/lifecycle verification and final integrated-artifact checks remain Main's separate responsibility.
