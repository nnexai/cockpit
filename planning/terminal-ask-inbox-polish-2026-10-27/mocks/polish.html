1 · Quick terminal (new terminal)

Before: 80%×80% of the work area, bare title and a large grey default-looking Close button, no scope or shortcut hint, status lines are unstyled rows that push the terminal down. After: bounded size, pane-header geometry with the shared 2px accent top edge, cwd chip, reserved status slot, Close with its shortcut. Close still confirms (DECISIONS.md:33).

3 · Agents queue: idle leaves on its own, nothing to dismiss

Herdr: Done = finished and not yet seen; Idle = seen / waiting (herdr.dev/docs/concepts). Queue shows Blocked, Working and unseen Done. An agent that is merely Idle, or whose result has been seen, is not listed. No hover button, no Delete-key dismissal, no hidden/restore/All machinery.
