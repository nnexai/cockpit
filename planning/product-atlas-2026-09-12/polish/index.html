Fix OMP terminal cell-width drift in Cockpit xterm

Summary

Cockpit’s xterm rendering of OMP showed a visible cell-metric mismatch against Herdr/Ghostty. The problem was most noticeable around the intentional blank cell between OMP’s inner vertical divider and the following horizontal rule.

Spaces, ordinary text, and box-drawing glyphs did not present a reliably uniform pitch.

User-visible symptom

  • The frame appeared less dense than the Herdr/Ghostty frame.
  • The blank cell after the inner divider looked wider than the neighboring cells.
  • Horizontal rules appeared offset even with the same one-cell separator.
